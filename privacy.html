Privacy Policy

ZipTrail, Rani, Cozy Passport, ShortStack, Updo: Kanzashi Hit, Stretchy Frog and Numory · Effective: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how Shiva Suthapalli, the developer of the games ZipTrail, Rani, Cozy Passport, ShortStack, Updo: Kanzashi Hit, Stretchy Frog and Numory (“the games,” “we,” “us”), handles information when you use any of the games on iPhone or Android. The games use this common privacy approach, though particular features below may be available only in some games.

Short version: The games have no accounts and no servers of their own. Your progress and settings stay on your device. Where enabled, ads are served by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Apple or Google with RevenueCat. Where offered, reminders are scheduled on your device. In games other than Numory, adults outside Europe may generate anonymous gameplay events with Google Analytics for Firebase. We don't sell your information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, hints, daily-puzzle streak, score, sakura, kanzashi, sound and reminder settings, and which purchases you own are saved only on your device. We don't receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Your device's own backups (iCloud or Google) may include them, according to your device settings.

In Stretchy Frog, your age-group choice, score, progress, fireflies, unlocked frog looks, settings, and locally verified review access are also stored only on your device. The age-group choice is not your date of birth.

In Numory, your score and progress stay on your device unless you choose to sign in to Google Play Games for leaderboards. If you do, Numory sends your score to Google Play Games. Numory does not create an account of its own.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Each game asks for a broad age group before starting advertising services. The choice is saved only on your device; we do not ask for your birth date or send your age group to an advertising provider. Players under 13 receive no ad requests. For players aged 13–17 and players who choose not to disclose their age, the app marks every ad request for Google's teen treatment: ads are not personalised, no consent form is shown, and ad content is limited to teen-appropriate material. Adults (18+) receive standard ads, subject to the consent choices below.

In games that offer rewarded videos, players aged 13 and over can choose one for an in-game benefit, such as a hint, continue or gift. Eligible players may also see an occasional full-screen ad at selected points in play. To deliver and measure these ads, Google AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier (on Android), IP address, general location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and your consent choices.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before requesting ads. You can change your choices at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, the games do not ask for permission to track you and do not access the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases (Apple, Google, RevenueCat)

Apple or Google processes payments for Remove Ads and optional game items, such as Solutions, Premium Kanzashi or Premium Frogs. We never receive your card or bank details. When the purchase service connects, RevenueCat creates an anonymous app user identifier. RevenueCat receives purchase and transaction information to confirm and restore purchases and prevent fraud. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. An access code supplied for store review is verified and stored locally, separately from purchases.

4. Reminders

In games that offer daily reminders, if you turn them on they are scheduled by your device's own notification system. Nothing is sent to us. The app asks for notification permission only after you choose to be reminded, and you can turn reminders off in Settings at any time.

5. Sharing

When you share an invite or game result, your device's share sheet sends it to the app you choose. The games do not access your contacts.

6. Analytics and diagnostics

In games other than Numory, if you chose 18+ and you are outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics for Firebase counts how you play: finishing the tutorial; starting and finishing levels, stages or runs (with score, stars, hints used and time taken); solving the daily puzzle; using hints; ads shown; which purchase offers you see; whether you tap Buy or Restore and how the store answers; and purchases. Firebase receives these events with an app-instance identifier, basic device and app information, and a general location derived from your IP address. It does not collect your advertising ID or Android ID, and it is not used for advertising. It stays off for players who chose a younger age group or prefer not to say, and in those regions. Google keeps this data for up to 14 months. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

For the same players in those games, under the same conditions, Firebase Crashlytics may receive crash reports when something goes wrong: the error, the app version, the device model and operating system version, and the last few game events listed above, with an installation identifier. They help us fix crashes. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

AdMob may collect diagnostics when advertising is enabled, as described above. Apple and Google may provide us with aggregated crash and performance reports if you have allowed this in your device settings.

7. Teens and younger players

The games are designed for teens ages 13+ and adults. They are not directed to children under 13. Advertising services remain disabled for players who select under 13; teens and players who don't disclose their age receive only non-personalised, teen-appropriate ads. Where enabled, Firebase Analytics runs only for players who chose 18+. Numory does not use Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

8. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

Where consent is required for advertising, adults see Google's consent form, which lets them choose whether to allow personalised ads and related processing. You can update your choices in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Declining personalised ads does not prevent you from playing. Purchase information is processed to provide and restore the features you buy.

9. Your rights and data-deletion requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data, and to complain to a data-protection authority. The games do not offer user accounts or an automated data-deletion service.

To request help deleting data for ZipTrail, Rani, Cozy Passport, ShortStack, Updo: Kanzashi Hit, Stretchy Frog or Numory:

  1. Uninstall the game to remove its local progress and settings. Check your device's backup settings separately if you also want to remove a saved backup.
  2. Email cynodev256@gmail.com with the subject “Game data-deletion request,” the game's name, and whether you use iPhone or Android. Do not send passwords, card details or bank details.
  3. We will help identify which provider-held records the request concerns and explain whether we can handle the request or you need to contact the provider directly. Anonymous records may require an identifier before a provider can locate them.

Numory players who use the optional Play Games leaderboards can also delete Numory Play Games data through Google Play Games. This does not delete records held by the game developer or other providers.

Uninstalling does not delete advertising, analytics, crash or purchase records held by Google, Apple or RevenueCat. Analytics and crash-report retention periods are described in section 6. Advertising and purchase records follow the providers' linked privacy policies and retention requirements; we cannot promise a fixed deletion period for records they control. Deleting purchase records may affect the ability to restore a purchase.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes; the effective date above will change with it. Questions or requests: cynodev256@gmail.com.
