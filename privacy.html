Privacy Policy

ZipTrail · Effective: October 7, 2026

This policy explains how Shiva Suthapalli, the developer of ZipTrail (“ZipTrail,” “we,” “us”), handles information when you use the ZipTrail puzzle game for iPhone and Android.

Short version: ZipTrail has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress, settings and streak stay on your device. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Apple or Google with RevenueCat. Reminders are scheduled on your device. We don't sell your information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, hints, daily-puzzle streak, sound and reminder settings, and which purchases you own are saved only on your device. We don't receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Your device's own backups (iCloud or Google) may include them, according to your device settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

ZipTrail shows a video when you ask for one (to earn a hint) and, from level 12, an occasional full-screen ad between levels. To deliver and measure these ads, Google AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier (on Android), IP address, general location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions, and your consent choices.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before requesting ads. You can change your choices at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, ZipTrail does not ask for permission to track you and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases (Apple, Google, RevenueCat)

If you buy Remove Ads or Solutions, Apple or Google processes the payment. We never receive your card or bank details. RevenueCat, our purchase-management provider, receives purchase information (the product, transaction details and an anonymous app user identifier) so the app can confirm and restore your purchase. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

4. Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled by your device's own notification system. Nothing is sent to us. The app asks for notification permission only after you choose to be reminded, and you can turn reminders off in Settings at any time.

5. Sharing

When you share an invite or your daily result, your device's share sheet sends it to the app you choose. ZipTrail does not access your contacts.

6. Diagnostics

ZipTrail doesn't include a separate analytics or crash-reporting service. Apple and Google may provide us with aggregated crash and performance reports if you have allowed this in your device settings.

7. Children

ZipTrail is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

8. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

Where consent is required for advertising, Google's consent form lets you choose whether to allow personalised ads and related processing. You can update your choices in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Declining personalised ads does not prevent you from playing. Purchase information is processed to provide and restore the features you buy.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Because we hold no account or server data about you, most data can be removed by uninstalling the app. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, contact us and we will help you reach them.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes; the effective date above will change with it. Questions or requests: cynodev256@gmail.com.
