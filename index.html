Calm puzzle games.

ZipTrail

A one-line path puzzle. Connect the numbers in order and cover every square. Explore 300 levels across ten worlds, plus a fresh daily puzzle.

Rani

A crown logic puzzle. Place one crown in every row, column and colour, and never let two crowns touch. Travel through 300 levels across ten sketchbook chapters, plus a fresh daily puzzle.

Cozy Passport

A calm packing puzzle. Fit every souvenir into the suitcase, with no gaps and nothing left over. Travel through 300 trips across ten destinations, plus a fresh day trip every day.

ShortStack

A cosy pancake puzzle. Flip the stack with your spatula until it's sorted, smallest on top, in as few flips as you can. Serve 300 orders across ten recipes as your café grows, plus a fresh special every day.

Updo: Kanzashi Hit

A Japanese hairpin timing game. Stick kanzashi into a spinning bun, unlock a collection, and travel through twenty levels across four seasonal worlds.
